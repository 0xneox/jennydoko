# Playtest Script — 8-Year-Old Profile

## Overview
This script guides a real-device playtest with an 8-year-old child.
The goal is to identify where kids stall, get confused, or lose interest.

## Setup
1. **Device**: Mid-range Android/iOS phone (not a tablet — test the real form factor)
2. **Environment**: Quiet room, child seated comfortably, no time pressure
3. **Observer**: Sit beside the child, take notes, DO NOT coach or help unless they ask
4. **Recording**: Screen record the session if possible (with parental consent)

## Pre-Test Questions
Ask the child BEFORE starting:
- "Do you like puzzles?" (Yes / No / Maybe)
- "Have you played games on a phone before?" (Yes / No)
- "What's your favorite game?"

## Test Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Level 1 (Brand New Player)
**Goal**: Can they finish Level 1 unaided?

**Steps**:
1. Launch the app — does the story comic appear? Do they watch it or skip it?
2. Does the Level 1 tutorial auto-trigger? Do they follow it or skip it?
3. After the tutorial, can they solve Level 1 without help?
4. **Log**:
   - Time to complete Level 1: _______ seconds
   - Did they use any hints? (Y/N) — How many? ___
   - Did they make any mistakes? (Y/N) — How many? ___
   - Did they use undo? (Y/N)
   - Did they use restart? (Y/N)
   - Where did they stall? (describe)
   - Did they understand the rules after the tutorial? (Y/N)

### Scenario 2: Level 201 (Grumpy Cats)
**Goal**: Can they handle the cat mechanic?

**Steps**:
1. Skip to Level 201 (via level select or settings)
2. Does the chapter story modal appear? Do they read it?
3. Does the mechanic intro tutorial appear? Do they follow it?
4. Can they solve Level 201?
5. **Log**:

   - Did they understand the cat aura? (Y/N)
   - Did they try to place a puppy on a cat? (Y/N)
   - Did they use hints? (Y/N) — How many? ___
   - Did they understand the cat's personal space? (Y/N)
   - Where did they stall? (describe)

## Post-Test Questions
Ask the child AFTER playing:
- "Was it fun?" (Yes / No / Maybe)
- "What was your favorite part?" _______
- "What was confusing?" _______
- "Would you play it again?" (Yes / No / Maybe)
- "Was the cat scary or funny?" _______
- "Did the hints help?" _______

## Stall Point Logging
For each stall point, record:
- **Level**: _______
- **What happened**: _______
- **What the child said/did**: _______
- **How long they stalled**: _______ seconds
- **Did they recover on their own?** (Y/N)
- **Did they ask for help?** (Y/N)
- **Suggested fix**: _______

## Common Stall Points to Watch For
1. **Tutorial skip**: Did they skip the tutorial and then get confused?
2. **Cat placement**: Did they try to place a puppy ON the cat?
3. **Linked beds**: Did they not realize both beds share one puppy?
4. **Twin quota**: Did they try to place only 1 puppy per row?
5. **Hint usage**: Did they not notice the hint button?
6. **Undo/restart**: Did they not notice the undo/restart buttons?
7. **Mode confusion**: Did they understand the difference between modes?
8. **Star rating**: Did they care about the star rating?
9. **Heart loss**: Did the heart loss upset them?
10. **Combo counter**: Did they notice the combo counter?

## Red Flags (Stop Test)
- Child is visibly frustrated for >30 seconds
- Child wants to quit before completing Level 1
- Child doesn't understand the basic rules after the tutorial + 2 minutes of play
- Child can't figure out how to place a puppy after 1 minute of trying
