Privacy Policy — JennyDoko

Last updated: September 15, 2026

The short version

JennyDoko is an offline puzzle game. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. Everything the game remembers — your progress, settings, and stats — stays on your device.

What is stored on your device

The game saves the following locally on your device (never sent to us or anyone else):

  • Level progress and unlocked levels
  • Game mode, sound, and music settings
  • Daily challenge streaks and stats
  • Story and tutorial "seen" flags

This data lives in your browser's local storage (web) or the app's private storage (Android/iOS).

What we do NOT collect

  • No accounts or sign-ups
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising or ad identifiers
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, or files
  • No crash reporting sent to third parties

Children's privacy

JennyDoko collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The game can be played fully offline.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. On the web version, clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Changes to this policy

If JennyDoko ever adds online features (such as a leaderboard), this policy will be updated before those features ship, and any new data handling will be described here.
